How to read it

White keys repeat the pattern C D E F G A B, left to right, low to high. The black keys come in groups of two and three; C is the white key just left of a group of two. Middle C, called C4, is near the middle of the keyboard. Each note on the score sits higher on the lines as the key moves right.
