Make it easy

Drop in any recording you have: a song, a phone clip, you humming the tune. Soundwaves pulls out the melody, snaps it to the beat, moves it into one octave on the white keys, and adds it to the piece list so you can play it on Play live. A busy mix comes out approximate; a hummed tune comes out close.

Takes about ten seconds for a minute of audio.
