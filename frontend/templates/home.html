How it works

Your sheet music (PDF or MusicXML) is read into notes. Your playing is turned into notes by Transkun, a piano transcription model. The two are lined up note by note, so a wrong key, a skipped note, an extra note or a rushed bar is found where it happened. The teacher's notes are written by Claude from those findings and the one-line bar notes by Gemini; neither model ever hears the audio or decides what was right. Measured on real student recordings with hand-labelled mistakes: 190 of 191 caught, 98% of flags real. Don't have a piece yet? Add one.
